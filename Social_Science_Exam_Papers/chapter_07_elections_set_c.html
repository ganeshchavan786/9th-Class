OFFICIAL ANSWER KEY & STEP-BY-STEP EXPLANATIONS (SET C)
Q. No. Correct Curricular Reference & In-Depth Conceptual Solution
Q.1 (A) ECI symbols order criteria: a recognized National Party must demonstrate broad multi-state electoral footprint across at least 4 states in votes and seats.
Q.2 (A) EVMs are 100% standalone, battery-operated units manufactured by BEL and ECIL; their proprietary OTP chips are burned at the foundry and physically lack wireless transmitters or antennas.
Q.3 (A) Following the Supreme Court's *Lily Thomas* ruling (2013), convicted lawmakers serving 2+ years face immediate disqualification without any 3-month appeal stay.
Q.4 (A) Pursuant to the Supreme Court order in 2019, the ECI conducts mandatory manual VVPAT slip verification for 5 randomly selected polling stations in every assembly constituency.
Q.5 (A) While NOTA provides a symbolic democratic rejection, under current parliamentary election law, plurality rules: the candidate with the most votes after NOTA is declared elected.
Q.6 (A) The Returning Officer (RO) is the statutory head of the constituency under the RPA, responsible for accepting nominations, polling logistics, vote counting, and issuing the certificate of election.
Q.7 (A) The 48-hour campaign blackout enables electors to reflect quietly, insulating communities from last-minute high-decibel propaganda, hate speech, and canvassing.
Q.8 (A) The Supreme Court struck down the Electoral Bond Scheme in 2024, holding that anonymous political financing violates voters' fundamental Right to Information about funding sources.
Q.9 (A) BLOs are grassroots officials who visit local residences to verify entries, add new 18-year-old voters, and delete deceased names to maintain clean electoral rolls.
Q.10 (A) Form 17C records the exact vote tally at each polling station; copies signed by the Presiding Officer are given to all polling agents to verify against counting day tallies.
Q.11 (A) At least 50 mock votes are cast in front of candidate agents, VVPAT slips are matched against the display, the memory is cleared to zero, and the machine is sealed with paper seals.
Q.12 (A) The MCC strictly prohibits using places of worship for electioneering or using communal/religious appeals to harvest votes, preserving communal harmony.
Q.13 (A) EVM balloting units feature embossed Braille numerals (1 to 16) alongside candidate buttons, ensuring secret and independent voting for visually impaired citizens.
Q.14 (A) If an impersonator has already voted in a voter's name, the presiding officer verifies the true voter's identity and gives them a 'Tendered Ballot Paper' sealed in a special envelope.
Q.15 (A) FPTP is simple, provides clear local constituency accountability (citizens know their local MP), and avoids the extreme coalition instability of multi-member party-list PR.
Q.16 (A) Service voters (armed forces), polling staff on duty, and recently senior citizens (85+) and persons with benchmark disabilities can cast postal ballots under strict ECI supervision.
Q.17 (A) The Presiding Officer manages the booth, verifies voter entry, maintains the ballot register (Form 17A), supervises polling agents, and seals EVMs with green paper seals.
Q.18 (A) Election results can be challenged only via an Election Petition presented to the High Court of the state within 45 days of the declaration of results.
Q.19 (A) Section 126A of the RPA bans exit poll telecasts from the start of the first phase until half an hour after voting closes in the final phase to prevent bandwagon effects.
Q.20 (A) The Control Unit's Result door is sealed with a serialized green paper seal; agents sign across it, and tampering breaks the paper, ensuring results cannot be accessed before counting day.
Q.21 (A) Voter Information Slips distributed by BLOs contain the elector's photograph, polling booth number, and serial number, facilitating smooth check-in at the booth.
Q.22 (A) Undue influence includes threatening voters with physical harm, excommunication, caste boycott, or spiritual damnation to coerce their vote choice.
Q.23 (A) A single Balloting Unit accommodates 16 candidate buttons (including NOTA); up to 24 Balloting Units can be daisy-chained to a single Control Unit to handle up to 384 candidates.
Q.24 (A) Post-polling EVMs are sealed in Strong Rooms with double locks (one key with the RO, one with an assistant), guarded by central armed police with live CCTV monitors in candidate camps.
Q.25 (A) The ultimate test of democratic elections is peaceful turnover of power: losing incumbents honorably concede, respecting the sovereign will of the people.
