OFFICIAL ANSWER KEY & RAPID EXPLANATIONS (SET F)
Q. No. Correct Scientific Rationale & Formula Summary
Q.1 (B) Kelvin (K) is the SI base unit of temperature. 0°C corresponds to 273.15 K.
Q.2 (C) $1\text{ km} = 1000\text{ m} = 1000 \times 1000\text{ mm} = 10^6\text{ mm}$.
Q.3 (C) Mass has only magnitude with no directional dependence, making it a scalar quantity.
Q.4 (B) A standard school meter rule is graduated in millimeters; its smallest division is 1 mm (0.1 cm).
Q.5 (B) $T = 2\pi\sqrt{L/g} \propto \sqrt{L}$. If length increases by 4 times, $T$ increases by $\sqrt{4} = 2$ times.
Q.6 (C) Water has maximum density at 4°C: $1.00\text{ g/cm}^3 = 1000\text{ kg/m}^3$.
Q.7 (B) Density is mass per unit volume ($\text{kg/m}^3 = \text{kg}\cdot\text{m}^{-3}$).
Q.8 (B) A seconds pendulum takes 1 second to swing from one extreme to the other; its full oscillation time period is exactly 2.0 seconds.
Q.9 (B) A volumetric pipette has a calibration mark delivering an exact volume (25.0 mL) with high precision.
Q.10 (C) Leading zeros (0.0) are placeholders. '5', '0', '2', and the trailing '0' are significant (4 significant figures).
Q.11 (C) An object sinks when its density exceeds the density of water (Relative density $> 1$).
Q.12 (B) $\text{Least count} = \frac{1\text{ mm}}{100} = 0.01\text{ mm}$.
Q.13 (B) When the air collar is open, ample oxygen produces a roaring, non-luminous blue flame of complete combustion.
Q.14 (A) $1\text{ atm} = 760\text{ mm of Hg} = 1.013 \times 10^5\text{ Pa}$ (approx. $101.3\text{ kPa}$).
Q.15 (C) Controlled variables are kept strictly constant so that only the independent variable affects the dependent variable.
Q.16 (B) $T(K) = 25 + 273 = 298\text{ K}$.
Q.17 (C) $\text{Volume} = s^3 = (2\text{ cm})^3 = 8\text{ cm}^3$.
Q.18 (B) A beam balance compares an unknown gravitational mass against standard reference masses ($m_1 g = m_2 g \Rightarrow m_1 = m_2$).
Q.19 (B) Parallax is a personal observational error caused by the observer's line of sight not being perpendicular to the scale.
Q.20 (C) Micro is denoted by the Greek letter $\mu$ (mu) and represents $10^{-6}$.
Q.21 (A) $\text{Mass} = \text{Volume} \times \text{Density} = 50\text{ cm}^3 \times 0.8\text{ g/cm}^3 = 40\text{ g}$.
Q.22 (C) Newton ($\text{kg}\cdot\text{m/s}^2$) is a derived unit of force. Meter, second, and kilogram are base units.
Q.23 (B) Surface gravity on the Moon is about $1.63\text{ m/s}^2$, which is approximately $1/6\text{th}$ of Earth's $9.8\text{ m/s}^2$.
Q.24 (B) A hydrometer floats in liquids to directly measure relative density based on its depth of immersion.
Q.25 (A) $\text{VC} = 1\text{ MSD} - 1\text{ VSD} = 1\text{ mm} - 0.9\text{ mm} = 0.1\text{ mm}$.
