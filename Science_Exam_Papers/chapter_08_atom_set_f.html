OFFICIAL ANSWER KEY & RAPID EXPLANATIONS (SET F)
Q. No. Correct Scientific Rationale & Formula Summary
Q.1 (A) J.J. Thomson identified cathode rays as streams of negatively charged subatomic particles called electrons.
Q.2 (A) Eugen Goldstein discovered canal rays (anode rays) consisting of positive ions.
Q.3 (A) James Chadwick proved the existence of neutral neutrons by bombarding beryllium with alpha particles.
Q.4 (B) Massive deflections of alpha particles led Rutherford to discover the tiny, dense, positively charged nucleus.
Q.5 (B) An alpha particle consists of two protons and two neutrons, identical to a Helium-4 nucleus ($He^{2+}$, mass 4 u, charge $+2e$).
Q.6 (B) Capacity $= 2n^2 = 2(2)^2 = 2 \times 4 = 8$ electrons.
Q.7 (B) According to the Bohr-Bury octet rule, the outermost shell cannot hold more than 8 electrons.
Q.8 (A) K-shell holds 2, L-shell holds 8, and M-shell holds the 1 remaining electron ($2, 8, 1$).
Q.9 (B) Chlorine gains 1 electron to complete its octet: $\text{Valency} = 8 - 7 = 1$.
Q.10 (B) Argon has a completely filled octet in its valence shell, giving it zero chemical combining capacity (valency 0).
Q.11 (B) Mass number $A = Z + N$ (total number of protons and neutrons in the nucleus).
Q.12 (B) Isotopes have the same number of protons but different numbers of neutrons (e.g., $^{12}C$ and $^{14}C$).
Q.13 (B) Isobars share the same mass number $A$ (e.g., $^{40}_{18}Ar$ and $^{40}_{20}Ca$).
Q.14 (B) Natural chlorine contains $^{35}Cl$ (75%) and $^{37}Cl$ (25%), yielding weighted average $\frac{35\times 3 + 37\times 1}{4} = 35.5\text{ u}$.
Q.15 (B) Uranium-235 undergoes controlled nuclear fission in reactors, generating electricity.
Q.16 (A) Cobalt-60 emits high-energy gamma radiation used to destroy malignant tumor cells.
Q.17 (A) Iodine-131 is selectively concentrated by the thyroid gland, treating thyroid diseases.
Q.18 (B) Protium ($^1_1H$) has $Z=1$ and $A=1$, having $1-1=0$ neutrons.
Q.19 (B) An electron has a rest mass of $9.1 \times 10^{-31}$ kg, which is about $1/1836$ (or $1/1840$) of a proton's mass.
Q.20 (C) Neutrons are electrically neutral subatomic particles with zero net charge.
Q.21 (A) Niels Bohr postulated stable stationary quantum orbits in 1913 to resolve atomic stability.
Q.22 (B) Magnesium readily donates its 2 valence electrons from the M-shell to achieve an octet, giving valency 2.
Q.23 (B) Criss-crossing valencies ($Ca^{2+}$ and $Cl^{1-}$) yields $CaCl_2$.
Q.24 (B) Chemical reactivity depends on valence electronic structure, which is identical across isotopes.
Q.25 (C) Nucleons are protons plus neutrons, which equals the mass number $A = 14$.
