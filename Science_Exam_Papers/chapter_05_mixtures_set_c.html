(A) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of Assertion (A).
(B) Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true but Reason (R) is NOT the correct explanation of Assertion (A).

Q.16 Assertion (A): Common salt cannot be separated from its water solution by filtration.
Reason (R): Sodium and chloride ions in solution are smaller than 1 nm and easily pass through the pores of filter paper.
Q.17 Assertion (A): Pure substances possess sharp and fixed melting and boiling points.
Reason (R): In a pure substance, the chemical composition and intermolecular attractive forces are uniform throughout.
Q.18 Assertion (A): Brownian motion in colloids is caused by gravitational force.
Reason (R): Gravity pulls colloidal particles downwards towards the bottom of the container.
Q.19 Assertion (A): Water is considered a chemical compound rather than a mixture of hydrogen and oxygen gases.
Reason (R): Water has completely different properties from its constituent elements and its components cannot be separated by physical means.
Q.20 Assertion (A): Adding common salt to pure water increases its boiling point and decreases its freezing point.
Reason (R): Salt is an ionic electrolyte that dissociates into $Na^+$ and $Cl^-$ ions, altering the solvent's vapor pressure.

OFFICIAL ANSWER KEY & EXEMPLAR EXPLANATIONS (SET C)
Q. No. Correct Scientific Rationale & NCERT Exemplar Analysis
Q.1 (B) Heating chemically forms iron(II) sulfide ($Fe + S \rightarrow FeS$). Adding dilute acid produces toxic hydrogen sulfide gas ($FeS + 2HCl \rightarrow FeCl_2 + H_2S \uparrow$) with a characteristic rotten egg odor.
Q.2 (B) According to the Hardy-Schulze rule, multivalent counter-ions ($Al^{3+}$) neutralize the electric charge of colloidal clay particles, causing rapid flocculation and sedimentation.
Q.3 (B) Petroleum is a complex mixture of miscible hydrocarbons with differing boiling points separated by industrial fractional distillation columns.
Q.4 (B) $\text{Mass of solution} = 36 + 100 = 136$ g. $\text{Percentage} = \frac{36}{136} \times 100 \approx 26.47\%$.
Q.5 (B) Soap is an amphiphilic emulsifier: its hydrophobic tail dissolves in oil droplets while its hydrophilic head interacts with water, forming micelles that prevent droplet coalescence.
Q.6 (B) Sulfur is soluble in organic carbon disulfide ($CS_2$), whereas iron is insoluble, allowing physical separation of the mixture.
Q.7 (C) Copper sulphate forms a true homogeneous solution where solute ions are $< 1$ nm in diameter, too small to scatter visible light rays.
Q.8 (A) Electrophoresis is the migration of electrically charged colloidal particles toward the oppositely charged electrode in an electric field.
Q.9 (A) Simple distillation vaporizes water, which condenses into pure liquid in the receiver, leaving solid salt behind in the distillation flask.
Q.10 (B) Ammonium chloride sublimes upon heating; its vapors rise and cool on the colder funnel walls, forming solid sublimate crystals.
Q.11 (B) Clouds and fog consist of liquid water droplets dispersed in atmospheric air (gas).
Q.12 (B) Dissolution of gases in liquids is exothermic; increasing temperature drives dissolved gas molecules out into the vapor phase (e.g., boiling water drives out dissolved air).
Q.13 (A) Paper chromatography separates plant pigments based on differential partitioning between cellulose paper and a moving solvent (petroleum ether and acetone).
Q.14 (A) Butter is a water-in-oil emulsion / gel where liquid water droplets are dispersed throughout a solid fat matrix.
Q.15 (C) Diamond is an allotropic form of pure elemental carbon ($C$).
Q.16 (A) True solution particles are dissociated ions of sub-nanometer scale, passing unobstructed through cellulose filter pores.
Q.17 (A) Because all particles in a pure chemical species are identical, phase transitions occur at precise characteristic temperatures, unlike mixtures which melt over a range.
Q.18 (D) Assertion is false: Brownian motion is caused by continuous unbalanced thermal impacts of dispersion medium molecules, not gravity. Reason is a true physical fact.
Q.19 (A) Water ($H_2O$) is chemically bonded in a fixed 1:8 mass ratio, exhibits totally distinct chemical properties (extinguishes fire while $H_2$ burns and $O_2$ supports combustion), and requires electrolysis to split.
Q.20 (A) Dissolved non-volatile solutes lower vapor pressure, causing boiling point elevation and freezing point depression (colligative properties).
Q.21 (B) Sample 1 is a physical mixture; iron retains its magnetic properties and is separated cleanly by a magnet.
Q.22 (B) Sample 2 is a chemical compound ($FeS$). Chemical combination alters the magnetic property; iron atoms are chemically bonded and cannot be separated physically.
Q.23 (B) Free sulfur dissolves in organic $CS_2$. Evaporating the filtrate recovers rhombic sulfur crystals.
Q.24 (A) In Sample 1, free iron reacts with acid ($Fe + 2HCl \rightarrow FeCl_2 + H_2 \uparrow$), evolving odorless, combustible hydrogen gas.
Q.25 (B) Chemical synthesis forms new bonds, producing compounds with properties entirely distinct from the constituent elements.
