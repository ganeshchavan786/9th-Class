Q.1 (C) $a = F/m = 12/3 = 4\text{ m/s}^2$. Final velocity $v = at = 4 \times 4 = 16\text{ m/s}$. Kinetic energy $E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 = 0.5 \times 3 \times (16)^2 = 1.5 \times 256 = 384\text{ J}$.
Q.2 (B) Force $= \frac{\Delta p}{\Delta t} = n \times m \times v = 20 \times 0.030\text{ kg} \times 500\text{ m/s} = 300\text{ N}$.
Q.3 (C) Impulse $= |p_f - p_i| = |(-mv) - (+mv)| = 2mv$.
Q.4 (A) Thrust $F = u \frac{dm}{dt} = M_0 g \Rightarrow \frac{dm}{dt} = \frac{M_0 g}{u} = \frac{1000 \times 9.8}{2000} = 4.9\text{ kg/s}$.
Q.5 (A) $a = \frac{m_2 - m_1}{m_1 + m_2} g = \frac{6 - 4}{6 + 4} (9.8) = \frac{2}{10} (9.8) = 1.96\text{ m/s}^2$.
Q.6 (B) $N = m(g - a) = 70 \times (9.8 - 2.8) = 70 \times 7.0 = 490\text{ N}$.
Q.7 (B) In free fall, $a = g \Rightarrow N = m(g - g) = 0\text{ N}$ (weightlessness).
Q.8 (B) Impact velocity $v_1 = \sqrt{2gh_1} = \sqrt{2 \times 10 \times 5} = 10\text{ m/s}$ downward. Rebound velocity $v_2 = \sqrt{2gh_2} = \sqrt{2 \times 10 \times 3.2} = 8\text{ m/s}$ upward. Impulse $= m(v_2 - (-v_1)) = 0.2 \times (8 + 10) = 0.2 \times 18 = 3.6\text{ N}\cdot\text{s}$.
Q.9 (B) Limiting static friction $f_s = \mu N = 0.4 \times (10 \times 9.8) = 39.2\text{ N}$. Since the applied force (30 N) is less than 39.2 N, the block does not move ($a = 0$).
Q.10 (B) $p = \sqrt{2m E_k}$. For equal $E_k$: $\frac{p_1}{p_2} = \sqrt{\frac{m_1}{m_2}} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{4}} = \frac{1}{2}$.
Q.11 (A) $E_k = \frac{p^2}{2m}$. For equal $p$: $\frac{E_{k1}}{E_{k2}} = \frac{m_2}{m_1} = \frac{4}{1}$.
Q.12 (A) $m_1 u_1 = (m_1 + m_2)V \Rightarrow 1000 \times 20 = (1000 + 2000)V \Rightarrow 20000 = 3000 V \Rightarrow V = 20/3 \approx 6.67\text{ m/s}$.
Q.13 (C) Action and reaction forces act in exactly opposite directions along the same line of action (180°).
Q.14 (B) $F = \rho A v^2 = 1000\text{ kg/m}^3 \times (10 \times 10^{-4}\text{ m}^2) \times (15\text{ m/s})^2 = 1 \times 225 = 225\text{ N}$.
Q.15 (B) Due to inertia of motion, the released stone inherits the balloon's instantaneous velocity of $5\text{ m/s}$ upward.
Q.16 (A) $E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 = \frac{(mv)^2}{2m} = \frac{p^2}{2m}$.
Q.17 (B) $F_c = \frac{m v^2}{r} = \frac{5 \times (4)^2}{2} = \frac{5 \times 16}{2} = 40\text{ N}$ directed radially towards the center.
Q.18 (C) $g = \frac{GM}{R^2}$. If $R \rightarrow R/2$, $g' = \frac{GM}{(R/2)^2} = 4g$.
Q.19 (A) $F = \frac{\Delta p}{\Delta t} = \frac{0.150\text{ kg} \times 20\text{ m/s}}{0.1\text{ s}} = \frac{3.0}{0.1} = 30\text{ N}$.
Q.20 (A) Newton's third law: The backward force exerted by the feet on water produces an equal forward reaction push on the duck.
Q.21 (C) $E_k \propto p^2$. If $p' = 1.5 p$, $E_k' = (1.5)^2 E_k = 2.25 E_k$. Percentage increase $= (2.25 - 1) \times 100 = 125\%$.
