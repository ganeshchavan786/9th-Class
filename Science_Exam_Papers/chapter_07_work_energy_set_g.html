Q. No. Correct Rigorous Scientific Solution & Formula Derivation
Q.1 (B) $W = \int_0^3 4x dx = [2x^2]_0^3 = 2(3)^2 = 2 \times 9 = 18\text{ J}$.
Q.2 (A) $v_{top} = \sqrt{gr} = \sqrt{9.8 \times 1} \approx 3.13\text{ m/s}$.
Q.3 (B) $v_{bottom} = \sqrt{5gr} = \sqrt{5 \times 9.8 \times 1} = \sqrt{49} = 7.0\text{ m/s}$.
Q.4 (A) $E_p = \frac{1}{2} k x^2 = 0.5 \times 200 \times (0.10)^2 = 100 \times 0.01 = 1.0\text{ J}$.
Q.5 (A) $P = \frac{mgh}{t} = \frac{800 \times 9.8 \times 15}{20} = \frac{117600}{20} = 5880\text{ W}$ ($5.88\text{ kW}$).
Q.6 (B) Energy $= 1.5\text{ kW} \times 4\text{ h} \times 30 = 180\text{ kWh}$. Cost $= 180 \times 6 = \text{₹}1080$.
Q.7 (A) Work-energy theorem: $F \times s = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 \Rightarrow F \times 0.20 = 0.5 \times 0.010 \times (400)^2 = 0.005 \times 160000 = 800\text{ J} \Rightarrow F = 800/0.20 = 4000\text{ N}$.
Q.8 (B) $\text{VR} = 5$. $\text{MA} = \eta \times \text{VR} = 0.80 \times 5 = 4.0$. Effort $= \frac{\text{Load}}{\text{MA}} = \frac{1000}{4.0} = 250\text{ N}$.
Q.9 (A) $p = \sqrt{2m E_k}$. If $E_k$ increases by 9 times, $p$ increases by $\sqrt{9} = 3$ times.
Q.10 (A) Centripetal force is perpendicular to the displacement at every point along the circular trajectory ($W = 0$).
Q.11 (C) Velocity at bottom: $v = \sqrt{2gL}$. Tension $T = mg + \frac{mv^2}{L} = mg + \frac{m(2gL)}{L} = 3mg$.
Q.12 (A) $E_k = \frac{p^2}{2m}$. For equal $p$, kinetic energy is inversely proportional to mass; the lighter mass has greater $E_k$.
Q.13 (A) $P = \frac{mgh}{t} = \frac{7200 \times 10 \times 10}{3600\text{ s}} = 200\text{ W}$.
Q.14 (B) Load arm $= 30\text{ cm}$. Effort arm $= 150 - 30 = 120\text{ cm}$. $\text{MA} = \frac{\text{Effort arm}}{\text{Load arm}} = \frac{120}{30} = 4$.
Q.15 (B) Friction dissipates mechanical energy into heat; its work depends on path length, making it non-conservative.
Q.16 (A) $F = mg\sin\theta = 1000 \times 9.8 \times (1/20) = 490\text{ N}$. Power $P = F v = 490 \times 15 = 7350\text{ W}$ ($7.35\text{ kW}$).
Q.17 (A) For $0 \le \theta < 90^\circ$, $\cos\theta > 0$, making work done positive.
Q.18 (B) $\frac{1}{2}m v_{max}^2 = \frac{1}{2}k A^2 \Rightarrow v_{max} = A\sqrt{k/m} = 0.2\sqrt{50/0.5} = 0.2\sqrt{100} = 0.2 \times 10 = 2.0\text{ m/s}$.
Q.19 (A) $500\text{ kWh} = 500 \times 3.6 \times 10^6\text{ J} = 1.8 \times 10^9\text{ J}$ (1.8 GigaJoules).
Q.20 (A) Gravity is a conservative force; the net work done along any closed path is zero.
Q.21 (A) $\text{MA} = \frac{\text{Effort arm}}{\text{Load arm}} = \frac{12\text{ cm}}{2\text{ cm}} = 6$.
Q.22 (A) Energy retained $= \frac{1.28}{2.00} = 64\%$. Energy dissipated $= 100 - 64 = 36\%$.
Q.23 (A) $W = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{s} = (3 \times 2) + (4 \times 5) = 6 + 20 = 26\text{ J}$.
Q.24 (A) Work must be done against electrostatic repulsive forces to bring like charges closer, increasing electrostatic potential energy.
Q.25 (A) $P = W/t = 1000/2 = 500\text{ W}$.
