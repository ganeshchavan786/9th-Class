OFFICIAL ANSWER KEY & RAPID EXPLANATIONS (SET F)
Q. No. Correct Scientific Rationale & Formula Summary
Q.1 (B) Robert Hooke observed dead honey-comb compartments in cork using an early microscope and coined the term 'cell'.
Q.2 (B) Anton van Leeuwenhoek improved microscopic lenses and discovered living microorganisms.
Q.3 (A) Rudolf Virchow expanded the cell theory in 1855 by stating that new cells arise from pre-existing cells by division.
Q.4 (B) The primary structural component of plant cell walls is cellulose, a fibrous carbohydrate polymer.
Q.5 (B) Mitochondria generate cellular energy in the form of ATP through aerobic cellular respiration.
Q.6 (B) Ribosomes are the macromolecular protein-synthesizing factories found in all living cells.
Q.7 (B) Lysosomes contain hydrolytic digestive enzymes that digest the cell itself if damaged, earning the name 'suicide bags'.
Q.8 (C) Chloroplasts contain chlorophyll pigments and thylakoid membranes that capture light to perform photosynthesis.
Q.9 (B) Smooth ER lacks ribosomes and specializes in lipid/steroid biosynthesis and hepatic detoxification.
Q.10 (A) Camillo Golgi discovered the Golgi apparatus, which packages and modifies proteins for secretion.
Q.11 (B) Water exits via exosmosis, causing the protoplast to shrink away from the cell wall (plasmolysis).
Q.12 (C) Osmosis is the net movement of water molecules across a selectively permeable membrane along a concentration gradient.
Q.13 (C) Prokaryotes lack a nuclear membrane; their genetic material lies naked in a region called the nucleoid.
Q.14 (A) Both mitochondria and chloroplasts contain circular DNA and 70S ribosomes, reflecting endosymbiotic prokaryotic origin.
Q.15 (A) The large central sap vacuole maintains hydrostatic turgor pressure against the plant cell wall.
Q.16 (B) Plasma membrane flexibility enables Amoeba to form pseudopodia and engulf food via endocytosis.
Q.17 (B) Chromatin consists of double-stranded DNA coiled around basic histone protein octamers.
Q.18 (B) The selectively permeable membrane surrounding the large central plant vacuole is the tonoplast.
Q.19 (C) Centrosomes and centrioles are animal cell organelles that organize spindle fibers during mitotic division.
Q.20 (A) Safranin stains plant cell walls and nuclei bright pink/red for clear microscopic examination.
Q.21 (A) ATP stands for Adenosine Triphosphate, the universal chemical energy storage molecule of cells.
Q.22 (A) Human erythrocytes have an average diameter of approximately 7.2 to 7.8 micrometers (μm).
Q.23 (A) The nucleolus is the dense subnuclear site of ribosomal RNA (rRNA) transcription and ribosome subunit assembly.
Q.24 (C) Amyloplasts are specialized non-pigmented leucoplasts that synthesize and store starch in storage organs.
Q.25 (B) Singer and Nicolson proposed the Fluid Mosaic Model: a phospholipid bilayer with embedded floating proteins.
