OFFICIAL ANSWER KEY & DETAILED EXPLANATIONS
Q. No. Correct Conceptual Explanation & Syllabus Rationale
Q.1 (B) Anton van Leeuwenhoek (1674) observed living cells in pond water. Robert Hooke (1665) had observed dead cork cells.
Q.2 (C) Rudolf Virchow (1855) added the crucial tenet that all cells arise from pre-existing living cells by division.
Q.3 (B) The cell membrane regulates transport, allowing essential nutrients and water to enter while blocking harmful or unwanted substances.
Q.4 (B) In a hypotonic solution (lower solute concentration outside), water enters the cell via endosmosis. Lacking a rigid cell wall, animal cells swell and can burst (lysis).
Q.5 (B) Plasmolysis is the withdrawal of protoplasm from the cell wall when a plant cell loses water in a hypertonic medium.
Q.6 (B) Smooth ER synthesizes lipids and steroids, and plays a major detoxifying role in liver cells. Rough ER synthesizes proteins.
Q.7 (B) Camillo Golgi described the Golgi apparatus, which modifies, packages, and routes proteins and complex biochemicals.
Q.8 (B) Lysosomes contain strong hydrolytic digestive enzymes. If the cell undergoes severe damage, lysosomes rupture, causing autolysis (self-digestion).
Q.9 (A) Mitochondria and plastids (chloroplasts) are semi-autonomous organelles with their own circular DNA and ribosomes.
Q.10 (B) Folds (cristae) provide a huge surface area for enzymes associated with the electron transport chain and ATP synthesis.
Q.11 (C) Leucoplasts are non-pigmented plastids whose primary function is nutrient storage (starch in amyloplasts, oils in elaioplasts).
Q.12 (B) Cellulose provides mechanical tensile strength and structural support to plant cell walls. (Chitin is found in fungal cell walls).
Q.13 (B) The central vacuole occupies 50-90% of the volume of mature plant cells, exerting turgor pressure against the cell wall.
Q.14 (B) Mitosis is equational division where chromosome number is conserved, resulting in two identical daughter cells for somatic growth and repair.
Q.15 (B) Meiosis produces 4 gamete cells, each carrying half ($n$) the original chromosome number of the diploid ($2n$) parent cell.
Q.16 (A) The strong cell wall exerts wall pressure equal and opposite to turgor pressure, preventing plant cells from bursting in hypotonic media.
Q.17 (A) Mitochondria carry out cellular respiration, synthesizing ATP which fuels virtually all endergonic biochemical processes.
Q.18 (C) Assertion is true (ribosomes translate mRNA into proteins). Reason is false because ribosomes are non-membrane-bound ribonucleoprotein complexes.
Q.19 (A) Chromatin condenses into tightly coiled discrete chromosomes only during prophase of cell division.
Q.20 (D) Assertion is false because prokaryotes lack a nuclear membrane and contain an undefined nucleoid. Reason is true.
Q.21 (B) Distilled water is hypotonic relative to raisin interior; water molecules enter raisins by endosmosis.
Q.22 (B) The concentrated salt solution has lower water chemical potential (hypertonic), causing water to flow out (exosmosis).
Q.23 (B) The selectively permeable plasma membrane regulates water movement via osmosis.
Q.24 (B) Boiling denatures proteins and destroys membrane integrity, rendering the cell non-living and unable to exhibit selective osmosis.
Q.25 (B) In an isotonic medium, the rate of water entering equals the rate of water leaving; net osmotic change is zero.
