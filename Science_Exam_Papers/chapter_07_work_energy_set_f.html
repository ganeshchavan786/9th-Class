OFFICIAL ANSWER KEY & RAPID EXPLANATIONS (SET F)
Q. No. Correct Scientific Rationale & Formula Summary
Q.1 (B) The Joule (J) is the SI unit of both work and energy ($1\text{ J} = 1\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$).
Q.2 (C) $W = F s \cos\theta$. When $\theta = 90^\circ$, $\cos 90^\circ = 0$, so work done is zero.
Q.3 (C) Gravity acts vertically downward while displacement is horizontal ($\theta = 90^\circ$; $W = 0$).
Q.4 (B) Kinetic energy is given by $E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$.
Q.5 (A) $E_p = mgh$, where $g$ is the acceleration due to gravity.
Q.6 (B) $E_k \propto v^2$. If speed doubles ($2v$), kinetic energy quadruples ($2^2 = 4$ times).
Q.7 (B) $1\text{ commercial unit} = 1\text{ kilowatt-hour (kWh)} = 3.6 \times 10^6\text{ J}$.
Q.8 (B) $1\text{ kWh} = 1000\text{ W} \times 3600\text{ s} = 3.6 \times 10^6\text{ J}$ (3.6 MegaJoules).
Q.9 (B) Power is the rate of doing work ($P = W/t$). Its SI unit is the Watt ($1\text{ W} = 1\text{ J/s}$).
Q.10 (B) One standard imperial mechanical horsepower equals exactly 746 Watts.
Q.11 (B) Displacement is upward while gravity acts downward ($\theta = 180^\circ$; $\cos 180^\circ = -1$; $W < 0$).
Q.12 (B) In the absence of dissipative friction, total mechanical energy ($E_k + E_p$) remains constant.
Q.13 (B) Work done in deforming the spring against elastic restoring forces is stored as elastic potential energy.
Q.14 (A) $\text{Energy} = P \times t = 0.060\text{ kW} \times 10\text{ h} = 0.6\text{ kWh}$.
Q.15 (B) By conservation of energy: $E_k = E_p = mgh = 2 \times 9.8 \times 10 = 196\text{ J}$.
Q.16 (B) $W_{net} = \Delta E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 - \frac{1}{2}mu^2$.
Q.17 (B) Stored water potential energy converts to kinetic energy as it rushes down, which spins turbines generating electricity.
Q.18 (B) Work output can never exceed work input without violating the law of conservation of energy.
Q.19 (B) $\text{Mechanical Advantage} = \frac{\text{Load (Resistance)}}{\text{Effort (Applied force)}}$.
Q.20 (B) In a wheelbarrow, the load is between the fulcrum (wheel axle) and the effort (handles), defining Class 2.
Q.21 (A) In a single fixed pulley, the effort moves the exact same distance as the load ($\text{VR} = 1$).
Q.22 (A) Centripetal gravitational pull is orthogonal ($ heta = 90^\circ$) to the satellite's instantaneous displacement ($W = 0$).
Q.23 (B) $P = \frac{W}{t} = \frac{F \times s}{t} = \frac{1000 \times 5}{10} = \frac{5000}{10} = 500\text{ W}$.
Q.24 (B) Kinetic friction always opposes the direction of sliding displacement ($ heta = 180^\circ$, $W < 0$).
Q.25 (A) $\text{Efficiency } \eta = \frac{\text{MA}}{\text{VR}} \Rightarrow \text{MA} = \eta \times \text{VR}$.
