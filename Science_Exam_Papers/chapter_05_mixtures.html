OFFICIAL ANSWER KEY & DETAILED EXPLANATIONS
Q. No. Correct Conceptual Explanation & Syllabus Rationale
Q.1 (B) Brass is a solid solution (alloy) having uniform composition and properties throughout, making it a homogeneous mixture.
Q.2 (B) $\text{Mass of solution} = 40 + 360 = 400$ g. $\text{Percentage} = \frac{40}{400} \times 100 = 10.0\%$.
Q.3 (C) In a true solution, solute particles are $< 1$ nm, pass through filter pores, do not scatter light, and never settle under gravity.
Q.4 (B) Colloidal particles are large enough (1-100 nm) to scatter visible light rays, illuminating the path of the beam (Tyndall effect).
Q.5 (C) Milk is a colloidal emulsion of fat and protein droplets in water, which scatters light and shows Tyndall effect.
Q.6 (A) In fog and mist, tiny liquid water droplets (dispersed phase) are dispersed in air/gas (dispersion medium).
Q.7 (B) Centrifugation spins mixtures at high speed, forcing denser particles to the bottom (used for separating cream from milk and blood diagnostics).
Q.8 (B) When boiling point difference is less than 25 K (or 25°C), fractional distillation with a fractionating column is required.
Q.9 (B) Ammonium chloride sublimes directly into vapor upon heating, leaving non-sublimable sodium chloride behind.
Q.10 (C) Solutes with higher solubility travel faster with the solvent front up the chromatography paper, separating into distinct bands.
Q.11 (B) Evaporating to dryness can decompose salts (charring) and leaves soluble impurities behind, whereas crystallization produces pure crystals.
Q.12 (B) Immiscible liquids form distinct layers based on density and are separated using a separating funnel with a stopcock.
Q.13 (C) A colloid with gas dispersed in a liquid medium is termed a foam.
Q.14 (B) Suspended dust and smoke particles in air act as colloidal scattering centers, making the light beam visible.
Q.15 (B) A saturated solution can dissolve no more solute at that specified temperature.
Q.16 (A) Air has a uniform chemical composition of nitrogen, oxygen, argon, etc., without visible phase boundaries.
Q.17 (A) Because suspension particles are heavy ($>100$ nm), thermal molecular motion cannot overcome gravity, causing sedimentation.
Q.18 (D) Assertion is false (sugar particles pass through filter pores easily). Reason is true (true solution particle size $<1$ nm).
Q.19 (C) Assertion is true. Reason is false: The boiling points of nitrogen (-196°C), argon (-186°C), and oxygen (-183°C) differ by only 3°C to 13°C (much less than 100°C).
Q.20 (C) Assertion is true. Reason is false because iodine is the dissolved solid (solute) and alcohol is the dissolving liquid (solvent).
Q.21 (B) Chromatography separates mixtures based on relative affinity for the stationary phase (paper) versus mobile phase (solvent).
Q.22 (B) The dye components and spot positions of the cheque ink matched Pen Y identically.
Q.23 (B) The porous paper matrix through which the solvent migrates is the stationary phase.
Q.24 (B) Components with higher solubility in the solvent move faster and travel further along the paper.
Q.25 (B) Medical and forensic laboratories use chromatography to isolate and identify pharmaceutical compounds and toxins from blood.
