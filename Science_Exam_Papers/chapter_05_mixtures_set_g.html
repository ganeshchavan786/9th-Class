Q. No. Correct Rigorous Scientific Solution & Formula Derivation
Q.1 (A) $\text{Mass \%} = \frac{m_{solute}}{m_{solute} + m_{water}} \times 100 \Rightarrow 20 = \frac{50}{50 + m_{water}} \times 100 \Rightarrow 50 + m_{water} = 250 \Rightarrow m_{water} = 200\text{ g}$.
Q.2 (C) Paints are sols (solid pigment particles dispersed in liquid vehicle). Cheese, jelly, and butter are gels (liquid trapped in solid).
Q.3 (A) Nitrogen has the lowest boiling point ($-196^\circ\text{C}$) and distills off first at the top of the fractionating column.
Q.4 (A) 1. Sublimation separates $NH_4Cl$. 2. Dissolving remainder in water and filtering leaves sand on filter paper. 3. Evaporating water leaves pure salt.
Q.5 (A) According to the Hardy-Schulze rule, oppositely charged polyvalent ions ($SO_4^{2-}$) neutralize colloidal surface charges, inducing coagulation.
Q.6 (B) Dissolution of $CaO$ and $Ca(OH)_2$ is strongly exothermic; by Le Chatelier's principle, adding heat decreases solubility.
Q.7 (B) $R_f = \frac{\text{Distance travelled by solute}}{\text{Distance travelled by solvent front}} = \frac{0}{d_{solvent}} = 0.0$.
Q.8 (B) Sublimation requires absorption of latent heat to break intermolecular bonds without changing chemical identity.
Q.9 (B) At 80°C, total mass of solution $= 360 + 100 = 460\text{ g}$. A 230 g sample contains half the amounts: $180\text{ g}$ sugar and $50\text{ g}$ water. At 20°C, 50 g of water holds $100\text{ g}$ sugar. Precipitated crystals $= 180 - 100 = 80\text{ g}$.
Q.10 (B) The Tyndall effect illuminates the light path through milk, proving the presence of colloidal fat and protein micelles.
Q.11 (B) The column packing creates a thermal gradient where rising vapor undergoes multiple theoretical condensation-vaporization plates.
Q.12 (A) Cod liver oil dispersed in water is an emulsion (liquid dispersed in liquid).
Q.13 (A) Acetone and water are miscible with a boiling point difference $> 25^\circ\text{C}$ (44°C difference), suitable for simple distillation.
Q.14 (D) Variable composition, retention of individual constituent properties, and physical separability are hallmarks of a mixture.
Q.15 (A) $\text{Mass \%} = \frac{25}{25 + 175} \times 100 = \frac{25}{200} \times 100 = 12.5\%$.
Q.16 (D) Plasma is the fourth state of matter consisting of superheated ionized gas and free electrons (found in stars, lightning, neon tubes).
Q.17 (B) BEC forms when a low-density boson gas is cooled to near absolute zero ($< 10^{-6}\text{ K}$), collapsing atoms into a single quantum state.
Q.18 (B) $FeS + H_2SO_4 \rightarrow FeSO_4 + H_2S \uparrow$. Hydrogen sulfide smells like rotten eggs.
Q.19 (B) Diamond's rigid 3D covalent network requires massive thermal energy to break strong $C-C$ covalent bonds.
Q.20 (B) Gel electrophoresis separates charged biological macromolecules (proteins, DNA) based on molecular size and charge.
Q.21 (A) Butter is a water-in-oil emulsion / gel where liquid water droplets are dispersed within solid fat matrix.
Q.22 (A) Sublimation changes only the physical state from solid to vapor without altering chemical composition.
Q.23 (A) $\text{Volume \%} = \frac{10}{10 + 90} \times 100 = \frac{10}{100} \times 100 = 10\%$.
Q.24 (A) Fractional crystallization exploits differential temperature-solubility coefficients to selectively harvest separate crystalline phases.
Q.25 (A) Colored gemstone glasses and minerals are solid sols (solid particles dispersed in solid matrix).
