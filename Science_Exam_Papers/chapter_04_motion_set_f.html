OFFICIAL ANSWER KEY & RAPID EXPLANATIONS (SET F)
Q. No. Correct Scientific Rationale & Formula Summary
Q.1 (B) Displacement is the vector distance representing the straight-line directed path from start to finish.
Q.2 (B) Acceleration is rate of change of velocity: $[\text{m/s}] / [\text{s}] = \text{m/s}^2$.
Q.3 (B) Slope $= \frac{\Delta s}{\Delta t}$, which is the definition of speed.
Q.4 (B) Slope of $v$-$t$ graph $= \frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}$, which gives the acceleration.
Q.5 (B) Area $= v \times t$, which dimensional analysis and integration show equals displacement.
Q.6 (B) $a = \frac{v - u}{t} = \frac{20 - 0}{5} = 4\text{ m/s}^2$.
Q.7 (B) $72 \times \frac{5}{18} = 4 \times 5 = 20\text{ m/s}$.
Q.8 (C) After one full round, the athlete returns to the exact starting position; net displacement is zero.
Q.9 (C) An odometer measures distance covered in km; a speedometer measures instantaneous speed.
Q.10 (C) The third kinematic equation of uniformly accelerated motion is $v^2 - u^2 = 2as$ (or $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$).
Q.11 (B) At the peak, $v = 0$. Using $v = u - gt \Rightarrow 0 = 19.6 - 9.8 t \Rightarrow t = 19.6 / 9.8 = 2.0\text{ s}$.
Q.12 (B) A zero slope (horizontal line) on an $s$-$t$ graph means position is not changing with time; the object is at rest.
Q.13 (C) The instantaneous velocity vector is always directed tangentially to the circular trajectory.
Q.14 (A) When acceleration opposes the direction of velocity, speed decreases, termed retardation or deceleration.
Q.15 (A) If displacement is zero (round trip), $\text{average velocity} = 0$, but total distance $> 0$ gives a positive average speed.
Q.16 (B) Velocity is not changing; $\Delta v = 0 \Rightarrow a = 0$.
Q.17 (B) $s = \frac{1}{2}gt^2 = 0.5 \times 9.8 \times (3)^2 = 4.9 \times 9 = 44.1\text{ m}$.
Q.18 (C) Velocity specifies both magnitude (speed) and direction of motion, making it a vector.
Q.19 (B) The bob speeds up toward the center and slows down toward the extremes, undergoing non-uniform periodic acceleration.
Q.20 (B) $\text{Speed} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{100\text{ m}}{4\text{ s}} = 25\text{ m/s}$.
Q.21 (B) Average speed over equal distance segments is the harmonic mean: $v_{avg} = \frac{2 v_1 v_2}{v_1 + v_2}$.
Q.22 (C) Velocity is tangential while centripetal acceleration is directed radially inward; they are mutually perpendicular (90°).
Q.23 (B) $a = \frac{0 - 30}{15} = -2\text{ m/s}^2$. Retardation $= +2\text{ m/s}^2$.
Q.24 (B) Acceleration is constant: $a = \frac{dv}{dt} = 4\text{ m/s}^2$, representing uniform acceleration.
Q.25 (A) A straight-line $s$-$t$ graph passing through the origin indicates constant slope, meaning uniform speed.
