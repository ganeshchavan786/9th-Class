OFFICIAL ANSWER KEY & DETAILED EXPLANATIONS
Q. No. Correct Conceptual Explanation & Syllabus Rationale
Q.1 (B) $W = mgh = 15 \times 10 \times 1.5 = 225$ J.
Q.2 (B) Gravitational force is directed towards the Earth's center (perpendicular to tangential displacement, $\theta = 90^\circ$). Since $W = F s \cos 90^\circ = 0$, work done is zero.
Q.3 (B) $E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$. Since $E_k \propto v^2$, doubling speed ($2v$) makes kinetic energy $(2)^2 = 4$ times.
Q.4 (B) Friction acts in the direction opposite to displacement ($\theta = 180^\circ$). Therefore, $W = F s \cos 180^\circ = -F s$ (negative work).
Q.5 (A) $\text{Power} = \frac{\text{Energy}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{1000\text{ J}}{10\text{ s}} = 100$ Watts.
Q.6 (B) $1\text{ kWh} = 1000\text{ W} \times 3600\text{ s} = 3.6 \times 10^6$ J.
Q.7 (B) By conservation of mechanical energy, final $E_k = \text{initial } E_p = mgh = 2 \times 10 \times 20 = 400$ J.
Q.8 (B) Mechanical Advantage $= \frac{\text{Load}}{\text{Effort}}$. A machine with $MA > 1$ acts as a force multiplier.
Q.9 (A) Class 1 lever has the Fulcrum in the middle (e.g., seesaw, crowbar, scissors). Class 2 has Load in the middle; Class 3 has Effort in the middle.
Q.10 (B) In a wheelbarrow and nutcracker, the Load is positioned between the Fulcrum and the Effort (Class 2 lever, $MA > 1$).
Q.11 (A) $W = mgh = 200 \times 10 \times 30 = 60,000$ J. Time $t = 2 \times 60 = 120$ s. $P = \frac{60000}{120} = 500$ W.
Q.12 (B) Work done against restoring spring force is stored as elastic potential energy due to configuration change.
Q.13 (B) $W = F s \cos 60^\circ = 10 \times 5 \times 0.5 = 25$ J.
Q.14 (B) A chemical battery/cell converts stored chemical energy into electrical energy through redox reactions.
Q.15 (A) A single fixed pulley does not multiply force ($MA = 1$); it simply changes the direction of the applied effort to make lifting convenient.
Q.16 (A) Because displacement $s = 0$, scientific work $W = F \times s = 0$, despite physiological muscular fatigue.
Q.17 (A) In the absence of air resistance, mechanical energy $E_k + E_p$ is conserved; gravitational potential energy converts completely into kinetic energy.
Q.18 (A) In Class 3 levers, effort arm is shorter than load arm ($MA < 1$), requiring more effort force but providing precision and amplified displacement.
Q.19 (B) Both are true ($P = W/t = F \cdot v$), but the second statement is the definition rather than the derived proof of $F \cdot v$.
Q.20 (C) Assertion is true. Reason is false because kinetic energy is directly proportional to the square of velocity ($E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$).
Q.21 (B) Water elevated to height $h$ possesses gravitational potential energy given by $mgh$.
Q.22 (B) As water loses height, its potential energy is converted into kinetic energy of flowing water.
Q.23 (C) $\text{Energy per second} = mgh = 2000 \times 10 \times 50 = 1,000,000$ J/s $= 1000$ kW $= 1$ Megawatt.
Q.24 (B) A generator uses electromagnetic induction to convert rotational kinetic energy into electricity.
Q.25 (B) The law of conservation of energy states that energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed.
