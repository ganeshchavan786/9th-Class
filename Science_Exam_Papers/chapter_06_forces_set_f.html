OFFICIAL ANSWER KEY & RAPID EXPLANATIONS (SET F)
Q. No. Correct Scientific Rationale & Formula Summary
Q.1 (C) Newton defined mass as the quantitative measure of a body's inertia (resistance to change in motion).
Q.2 (B) Momentum $p = mv$, having units $[\text{kg}] \times [\text{m/s}] = \text{kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$ (or $\text{N}\cdot\text{s}$).
Q.3 (B) Newton's Second Law states that force equals rate of change of momentum: $F = \frac{\Delta p}{\Delta t} = ma$.
Q.4 (B) $F = ma = 5\text{ kg} \times 4\text{ m/s}^2 = 20\text{ N}$.
Q.5 (B) Action and reaction are simultaneous, equal in magnitude, opposite in direction, and act on two distinct interacting bodies.
Q.6 (B) Extending impact time $\Delta t$ drastically reduces peak impact force ($F = \Delta p / \Delta t$), preventing hand injury.
Q.7 (C) Due to inertia of direction, passengers tend to maintain their original straight-line direction of travel.
Q.8 (A) Dust particles tend to remain stationary due to inertia of rest while the carpet fabric is suddenly jerked away.
Q.9 (A) By conservation of momentum: $M V + m v = 0 \Rightarrow 4 V + (0.02 \times 400) = 0 \Rightarrow 4V = -8 \Rightarrow V = -2.0\text{ m/s}$.
Q.10 (D) Linear momentum is defined algebraically as $p = mv$.
Q.11 (B) $1\text{ N} = 1\text{ kg} \times 1\text{ m/s}^2 = 1\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}^2$.
Q.12 (B) High-speed exhaust gas ejected downward produces an equal and opposite forward reaction thrust, conserving momentum.
Q.13 (B) Newton's First Law: In the absence of an unbalanced net force, a body maintains constant velocity.
Q.14 (B) The foot pushes backward on the ground; static friction pushes forward on the foot, propelling the person.
Q.15 (B) Velocity is zero ($v = 0$); therefore, momentum $p = mv = 0$.
Q.16 (B) Soft foam increases deceleration time $\Delta t$, drastically reducing peak impact force on the athlete's body.
Q.17 (A) Impulse $J = F \Delta t = \Delta p$ (change in momentum).
Q.18 (C) The Law of Conservation of Linear Momentum states that total system momentum remains invariant without external forces.
Q.19 (B) Inertia is directly proportional to mass. Greater mass equals greater inertia.
Q.20 (B) $\Delta p = F \times \Delta t = 10\text{ N} \times 3\text{ s} = 30\text{ N}\cdot\text{s} = 30\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$.
Q.21 (B) Newton's First Law: Zero force is required to maintain uniform velocity in the absence of resistive friction.
Q.22 (B) The external forward reaction force exerted by the ground on the horse's hooves accelerates the system forward.
Q.23 (A) $\text{Mass} \times \text{Velocity} = [\text{M}] \times [\text{LT}^{-1}] = [\text{MLT}^{-1}]$.
Q.24 (B) $p = mv$. If $v$ doubles, momentum doubles.
Q.25 (A) The lower body moves forward with the bus while the upper body tends to remain at rest due to inertia of rest.
