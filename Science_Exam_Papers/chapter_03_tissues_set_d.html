SECTION A: CORE THEORETICAL PRINCIPLES (Q.1 TO Q.10) [10 MARKS]
Q.1

Assertion (A): Meristematic tissues in plants are characterized by continuously dividing, undifferentiated cells.

Reason (R): Meristematic cells possess dense cytoplasm, thin cellulose walls, prominent nuclei, and lack vacuoles.

Q.2

Assertion (A): Apical meristem is responsible for the increase in length of plant stems and roots.

Reason (R): Apical meristem is located at the growing tips of stems and roots and adds new primary cells longitudinally.

Q.3

Assertion (A): The girth (thickness) of the stem or root of a woody dicot plant increases due to lateral meristem (cambium).

Reason (R): Lateral meristems undergo periclinal cell divisions, adding secondary xylem and phloem radially to the vascular cylinder.

Q.4

Assertion (A): Sclerenchyma cells provide immense mechanical strength and rigidity to plant organs.

Reason (R): Sclerenchyma tissues are composed of dead cells with uniformly thick walls heavily impregnated with lignin.

Q.5

Assertion (A): Collenchyma provides both mechanical support and flexibility to young dicot stems and leaf petioles.

Reason (R): Collenchyma cells have localized wall thickenings of cellulose, hemicellulose, and pectin at their cell corners.

Q.6

Assertion (A): Xylem is classified as a complex permanent tissue in vascular plants.

Reason (R): Xylem consists of multiple distinct types of cells (tracheids, vessels, xylem parenchyma, and xylem fibres) working together as a functional unit.

Q.7

Assertion (A): Blood is classified as a connective tissue.

Reason (R): Blood has a fluid matrix called plasma in which RBCs, WBCs, and platelets are suspended, connecting organ systems via transport.

Q.8

Assertion (A): Cardiac muscle tissue can contract rhythmically throughout a person's life without ever experiencing muscular fatigue.

Reason (R): Cardiac muscle fibers are involuntary, cylindrical, branched, uninucleate, and richly supplied with mitochondria and capillaries.

Q.9

Assertion (A): Skeletal muscles are also termed voluntary striated muscles.

Reason (R): Their contractions are under conscious voluntary nervous control and display alternating dark and light bands under a light microscope.

Q.10

Assertion (A): Tendons connect bone to bone across a movable joint.

Reason (R): Tendons are tough, fibrous connective tissues with high tensile strength and limited elasticity.

SECTION B: EXPERIMENTAL OBSERVATIONS & INFERENCES (Q.11 TO Q.18) [8 MARKS]
Q.11

Assertion (A): Aquatic plants like Hydrilla and water hyacinth float effortlessly on the surface of water.

Reason (R): Parenchyma in aquatic plants modifies into aerenchyma containing large interconnected air cavities that provide buoyancy.

Q.12

Assertion (A): The husk of a dry coconut is extremely tough, stiff, and fibrous.

Reason (R): Coconut husk is made of dense sclerenchymatous fibres with heavily lignified, cemented cell walls.

Q.13

Assertion (A): Stomata in the leaf epidermis open and close during gas exchange and transpiration.

Reason (R): Each stomatal pore is bounded by two kidney-shaped guard cells whose turgor changes cause stomatal opening and closing.

Q.14

Assertion (A): Desert plants (xerophytes) have a thick waxy cuticle layer of cutin covering their outer epidermis.

Reason (R): The hydrophobic waxy cutin layer drastically minimizes excessive water loss through transpiration in arid environments.

Q.15

Assertion (A): A fractured bone can repair itself over time, whereas severe cartilage wear at joint surfaces heals very poorly.

Reason (R): Bone is richly vascularized with living osteocytes and blood capillaries in Haversian canals, whereas cartilage is largely avascular.

Q.16

Assertion (A): Ciliated columnar epithelium lines the human respiratory tract and fallopian tubes.

Reason (R): The rhythmic, coordinated beating of cilia moves mucus and trapped foreign particles outwards along the tract surface.

Q.17

Assertion (A): Mature sieve tube elements in phloem can conduct organic nutrients even though they lack a cell nucleus.

Reason (R): Each sieve tube element is intimately connected to an adjacent nucleated companion cell that regulates its metabolic activities.

Q.18

Assertion (A): Cork acts as an impervious protective outer covering on mature tree trunks.

Reason (R): The cell walls of mature dead cork tissue contain suberin, a chemical that makes them completely impermeable to water and gases.

SECTION C: ADVANCED NUANCES & MISCONCEPTIONS (Q.19 TO Q.25) [7 MARKS]
Q.19

Assertion (A): Smooth (unstriated) muscles are present in the walls of the stomach, intestine, and blood vessels.

Reason (R): The contractions of visceral internal organs are involuntary and do not show cross-striations under a microscope.

Q.20

Assertion (A): Ligaments are strong, flexible connective tissues that hold bones together at joints.

Reason (R): Ligaments contain a high proportion of elastin fibres, imparting considerable elasticity while preventing joint dislocation.

Q.21

Assertion (A): Xylem parenchyma is the only living component in the xylem tissue of angiosperms.

Reason (R): Tracheids, vessels, and xylem fibres are all dead, lignified cells at functional maturity.

Q.22

Assertion (A): Nerve impulses travel rapidly over long distances across the human body.

Reason (R): Neurons possess a long transmitting cytoplasmic extension called the axon, often covered with an insulating myelin sheath.

Q.23

Assertion (A): Adipose tissue acts as an efficient thermal insulator beneath the skin.

Reason (R): Adipocytes store fat globules, and subcutaneous fat is a poor conductor of heat, preventing heat loss from the core body.

Q.24

Assertion (A): Intercalary meristem present at the base of leaves or internodes allows grasses to regenerate quickly after grazing by herbivores.

Reason (R): Intercalary meristem cells divide actively, regenerating lost shoot tissue from below the clipped tip.

Q.25

Assertion (A): Epithelial tissue forms a continuous protective sheet with no intercellular spaces or blood vessels.

Reason (R): Epithelial cells rest on an extracellular basement membrane that separates them from underlying connective tissue.
